## Your account

Keep your sign-in details private. You can delete your account at any time in the app under Settings, Delete account. Your data is removed within 30 days.

## Free and Pro plans

The free plan lets you send 3 documents a month. Pro is billed through your Apple Account and renews automatically until you cancel in your Apple subscription settings. One-time purchases do not renew. Refunds are handled by Apple under its own policies.
